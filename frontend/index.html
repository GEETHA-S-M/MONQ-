FAQ

Common questions

Monk fruit, also known as Luo Han Guo, is a small melon that has grown in the misty hills of Guangxi, China since the 13th century. monq. uses its natural sweetness — mogroside V — with nothing else added.

Use it exactly like sugar, gram for gram, in drinks, baking, or cooking. Because it’s concentrated, a little goes further than table sugar — start with less than you think.

Start with a 1:1 swap by volume for most recipes, then adjust to taste — monk fruit’s sweetness curve is a little different from cane sugar’s.
